# Kick-off - agreement acceptance at login (2026-05-12)

- Legal needs a defensible record that every ambassador accepted the current Brand Ambassador Agreement, Policies & Procedures and Privacy Policy.
- Rising PAGA and class-action suits against direct sellers make this urgent.
- Ambassadors accept right after login, on any device, English or Spanish.
- Until they accept, they cannot use the site.
- Record who accepted which version, when, from which IP and device.
- ARC (internal) logins must never accept on an ambassador's behalf.
- New versions must trigger re-acceptance automatically.
- US Ambassadors only for phase 1; VIP and Retail were discussed and excluded.
- Target: live before the end of Q3 2026.
