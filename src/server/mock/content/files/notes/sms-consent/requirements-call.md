# Requirements call - SMS consent capture (2026-09-24)

- Marketing wants to start SMS campaigns in Q1 2027; Legal says no marketing SMS without express written consent (TCPA).
- Capture consent at enrollment and at checkout with an unchecked checkbox and the approved disclosure text.
- Customers must be able to turn SMS off in My Account at any time.
- Replies STOP, END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE or QUIT must opt the number out immediately; HELP returns support info.
- Keep proof of consent: phone number, disclosure version, where it was captured, timestamp, IP.
- Order and shipping texts are transactional and stay separate from marketing consent.
- Legal is reviewing the disclosure wording in LEGAL-77.
- Double opt-in (reply YES to confirm)? Legal to decide.
- Existing customers who never consented get nothing until they opt in.
